FAQ

How many students per group?

Max 4 in-person. Online 1-on-1 or mini-groups of 3.

Do I need surf experience?

No. Punta de Lobos has beginner breaks + certified local instructors. Board & wetsuit included.

Where are classes?

Casa de Katta: terrace, kitchen & garden, 10 min walk to the beach. Fast WiFi for nomads.

How does booking work?

Add to order → send your booking via WhatsApp → we confirm availability within 24h → pay securely with a Flow or MercadoPago link. No prepayment on this site.

What study material do I get?

PDF guide (about 5 pages per class) sent by email, plus in-class practice with real Chilean situations.
